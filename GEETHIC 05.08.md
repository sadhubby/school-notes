## Ethics: The What and The Why
Ethical discussions are considered "soft, ambiguous or nebulous"
Many professions still find it difficult to clearly think about ethical issues

The Paradox: Ethics is enormously important but difficult to think about clearly and responsibly

Ethics may be defined as a practical and normative science. Based on reason, which studies human acts and provides norms for their goodness and badness.

Otherwise known as moral philosophy, insofar as it deals with morality, moral rectitude or the rightness and wrongness of human acts.
