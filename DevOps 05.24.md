### ITSM

IT Service Management

Also used in IT organizations. It can be business, so that it does not just support other organizations or business

ITSM is the activities that are performed by an orgnization to design, build deliver operate and control information technology services offered to customers

provides guidance and structure to processes change, configuration, release, incident and management.

ITSM processes underpin the entire service from strategy, design, transition, operations and improvement and value creation.
